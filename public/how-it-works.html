Privacy and trust

For everyone.

  • It never guesses about the talk. If the material doesn't say it, you're told so. How answers work
  • Anonymous by default. Asking needs no name. Details are asked only when you send to the speaker, and only the speaker sees them.
  • No tracking. No tracking cookies, no ads, no outside trackers.
  • Details don't linger. Names, emails and LinkedIn links are wiped 90 days after joining, or sooner if you delete them. Privacy
