Why Auto Q&A

A talk ends. Ten hands go up, there is time for three. Everyone else leaves with their question.

  1. Most questions never get asked

    No time, no microphone, too shy, wrong language.

    Everyone asks from their phone, with or without their name.

  2. Many are already in your slides

    "What was that number?" "Which study?"

    Answered straight away, with the slide shown.

  3. The best ones need you

    The slides can't know what you think, or what comes next.

    They wait on your phone. Reply when you can; everyone who asked sees it.

Who it's for

  • Conference speakersPapers, keynotes, panels
  • LecturersBig rooms, quiet students
  • ResearchersTalks, defences, posters
  • Meetups and trainingsMixed levels, many languages
  • Public meetingsPlans, consultations, town halls

What it isn't

  • It doesn't make things up about your talk. If your material doesn't say it, it says so and offers the question to you.
  • It doesn't replace you. You get the questions only you can answer.
  • No app, no sign-up for the audience, no tracking. How answers work

Questions

Does the audience need an account?

No. They scan the code and ask. Only you, the speaker, have an account.

Which files can I use?

PowerPoint, PDF and Word, plus notes you type or say. Keynote and Google Slides: export as PowerPoint first. Your slides are shown as pictures in the answers.

What does it read?

The words on your slides, your speaker notes and your answers to a few setup questions. It doesn't read charts or photos, so say what a chart shows in your notes.

Is it anonymous?

Asking is. If someone sends a question to you, they choose whether to add their name, email or LinkedIn. Other people in the room never see those.

What if an answer is wrong?

Answers are automated and can be wrong; each one shows the slide it comes from so people can check. Try your talk as the audience before you present, and add a note where it slips.

What does it cost?

Nothing while we're testing. If that changes, you'll be told before anything is charged.
