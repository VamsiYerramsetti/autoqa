What it isn't

  • It doesn't make things up about your talk. If your material doesn't say it, it says so and offers the question to you.
  • It doesn't replace you. You get the questions only you can answer.
  • No app, no sign-up for the audience, no tracking. How answers work

Questions

Does the audience need an account?

No. They scan the code and ask. Only the speaker has an account.

Which files can I use?

PowerPoint, PDF and Word, plus notes you type or say. From Keynote or Google Slides, export as PowerPoint first.

What does it read?

The words on your slides, your speaker notes and your answers to a few setup questions. Not charts or photos, so say what a chart shows in your notes.

Is it anonymous?

Asking is. Sending a question to the speaker, people choose whether to add their name, email or LinkedIn. Others in the room never see those.

What if an answer is wrong?

Answers are automated and can be wrong; each shows the slide it comes from so people can check. Try your own code first and fix any answer that slips.

What does it cost?

Nothing while we're testing. If that changes, you'll be told before anything is charged.
