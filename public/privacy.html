Auto Q&A

Privacy

Draft for testing. Needs a legal review before the product goes public.

If you ask a question

  • You can ask without giving your name.
  • Answers are written by an automated assistant using the speaker's slides. Your question is sent to Anthropic, the company whose model writes the answer.
  • If you send a question to the speaker, they get your name, email, your LinkedIn link if you add one, and the question. Nothing else. Others in the room see the question (never your name) so they can tap "Me too".
  • "Me too" needs no name or email. The speaker sees only how many people want an answer.

If you give a talk

  • We keep your name, email, your slides and your replies so the service can work.
  • Your slides are only used to answer your audience.

Cookies and tracking

  • No advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Your browser remembers your place in a talk, so a refresh doesn't lose your questions.
  • We count visits and questions to improve the product. The counts hold no names or emails and can't follow you from one day to the next.

Delete your details

This removes your name, email and questions for every talk you joined from this browser.
