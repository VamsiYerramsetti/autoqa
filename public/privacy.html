How answers are written, and who else handles data

Answers are generated automatically by a language model (Claude, made by Anthropic). Each time someone asks, the question is sent to Anthropic together with the speaker's material for that talk, and the answer comes back. The model is not trained on your questions or on anyone's slides: Anthropic's commercial terms do not allow it to train its models on this content. More on how answers work.

How long things are kept

  • Your name, email and LinkedIn link as an audience member: wiped automatically 90 days after you joined the talk. The question itself stays with the talk, without you attached.
  • Questions and answers: as long as the speaker keeps the talk. Deleting the talk or the account deletes them.
  • A speaker's account, slides and talks: until the speaker deletes them or closes the account ("Close my account" on the "Your talks" page), which removes everything at once.
  • Visit and usage counts: 365 days. They contain no names, emails or addresses.
  • Sign-in sessions: 30 days, then you sign in again.

How visits are counted, without cookies

When a page opens, it tells our server which kind of page it is (for example "audience chat"), the name of the website that sent you if there was one (only the site's name, never the full address), and your screen width so we can tell a phone from a computer. When you leave the page, it tells us how long the page was on your screen and how far down you scrolled. Your browser type and system (for example "Safari on iOS") are worked out from what every browser sends anyway.

To count each visitor once a day, we make a one-way code from a random number that is replaced every day, a shortened version of your internet address (the last part removed) and your browser type. Yesterday's random number is thrown away, so the code can't be turned back into your address or linked to another day. Your internet address itself is never stored. Nothing is stored on your device for this.

If your browser sends "Global Privacy Control" or "Do Not Track", none of this is sent and the server ignores you entirely. The counts are kept for 365 days, are seen only by the team running Auto Q&A, and are never shared or combined with anything else. Speakers testing their own talk are left out of all counts.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to: show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it, give it to you in a portable form, and stop using it where we rely on our legitimate interest. Where we rely on your consent you can withdraw it at any time. Write to the contact address above; we answer within one month. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in the Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

No decisions with legal or similarly significant effects are made about you automatically. The automated answers are information about a talk, nothing more.

Age

Speaker accounts are for people aged 16 or older. The audience side is meant for talks, lectures and conferences; we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has sent us their details, contact us and we will delete them.

Security

  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS), including between the website and its database.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted one-way scrambles; sign-in tokens are stored scrambled too.
  • A speaker's talk, slides and inbox can only be opened by that speaker.
  • The site sends strict browser protection rules, limits how fast anything can be asked or uploaded, and is checked for security problems daily.

Found a security problem? Please tell us at the contact address above before making it public. If personal data is ever exposed, we will tell the people affected and the data protection authority as the law requires.

Changes
