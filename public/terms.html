1. The service

Auto Q&A lets a speaker upload material for a talk and lets the audience ask questions about it. Answers are generated automatically from that material; questions the material does not cover can be sent to the speaker. The service is run by its founder, based in the Netherlands ("we"). Contact: the contact address will be listed here before public launch.

  • You can delete a talk or your whole account at any time, and the material goes with it.

4. Automated answers

  • Answers are produced by an automated system. They are labelled as automated and are based on the speaker's material; general background is marked as not coming from the talk.
  • They can be incomplete or wrong. Do not rely on them for medical, legal, financial or safety decisions. If it matters, ask the speaker.
  • Speakers: the answers are given on your behalf from what you provided. Check how your talk answers ("Try it as the audience") before you present.

We may remove material, close a talk or close an account that breaks these rules.

We look at every report and remove material that should not be there.
