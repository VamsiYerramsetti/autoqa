   observations. 0.82 on validation (daily discharge, 2006-2024) is good by common rules of thumb. Must be plain,
   kind, short; no hand-off needed (a quiet "ask Léa too" is tolerable but unnecessary). Slide 7.

   area. General knowledge, marked lightly. Slide 5. No hand-off. Should be short (it is a quick follow-up).

   rain, so water that used to come in summer arrives April-June; the peak month moves from July to June. It is snow
   timing, not more glacier melt. Should EXPLAIN (connect: glacier matters most in August, slide 3's 62% optional).
   Slide 11. No hand-off.
