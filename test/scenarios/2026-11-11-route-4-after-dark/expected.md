# Answer key (private): Route 4 after dark, 2026-11-11

Everything in this talk is made up. Slides are numbered as in `deck.pdf` (18 slides; `build-deck.mjs` rebuilds it).
Facts the speaker knows that are NOT on the slides: drivers keep their jobs with Tarn on the same pay; the phone line is
staffed by people 08:00 to 01:30 with two handlers in the evening, no smartphone or email needed; why Aldercote is left
out; bank holidays run to Sunday hours (all four are setup answers); standing bookings (pasted note). Bikes, the
contract penalty and the plan for 00:45 hospital finishes only arrive as the speaker's replies.
Slides that qualify each other: 11 (£32,000 saved) and 12 (about £28,700 if trips stay flat); 9 (aim: 9 in 10 within
15 minutes) and 14 (the trial is judged at 85%).
