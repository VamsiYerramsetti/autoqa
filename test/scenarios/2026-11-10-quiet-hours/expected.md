## Bram (night nurse, fragments, wary)

   25 minutes per nurse per night (Slide 12). Slide 6 and/or 12. No hand-off. Short and direct, no lecturing.
2. Alone with 9 patients, what do I do? The talk gives the facts (1 nurse to 9 at night, Slide 3; 25 min, Slide 12; call bells

   Should say the talk doesn't cover how to manage that and offer the hand-off. Acknowledging the worry briefly is good.
3. Why no bed alarms? From the speaker notes on Slide 6: left out on purpose; the 2024 Ward 4 trial had about 70% false
   alarms and staff stopped reacting. Slide 6. No hand-off.

6. Control ward? **No**: before-and-after only, no control ward (Slide 16). Good answers add that Ward 6 is proposed as a
   comparison for the next stage (Slide 17). No hand-off.
